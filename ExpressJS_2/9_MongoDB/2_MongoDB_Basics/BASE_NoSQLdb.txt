==============================
NoSQL BASE Properties
==============================

BASE stands for:
----------------
B - Basically Available:
    - System guarantees availability.
    - Every request gets a response (may be stale or partial).

S - Soft State:
    - Data may change over time, even without new updates.
    - Temporary inconsistency is allowed.

E - Eventual Consistency:
    - Eventually, all replicas converge to the same value.
    - Guarantees consistency over time, not immediately.

--------------------------
Example: Social Media Post (like Facebook)
--------------------------

Scenario:
- A person in India posts a photo.
- Database: NoSQL distributed DB (e.g., Cassandra, MongoDB).

1. Basically Available (B):
    - The post is acknowledged immediately: "Post uploaded!"
    - Even if USA servers are down, India servers accept it.

2. Soft State (S):
    - Post may not appear instantly to everyone.
    - Some users in India see it immediately, some see it after a few seconds.

3. Eventual Consistency (E):
    - After a short time, the post appears to all users globally (including USA).
    - All database replicas eventually have the same data.

--------------------------
Key Idea:
BASE = Fast responses + temporary inconsistencies + eventual global consistency.
Perfect for highly distributed apps like social media platforms.
